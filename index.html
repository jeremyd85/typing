          settings

          text

          words are drawn from the most common English words. sentences are whole sentences with their capitals and punctuation.

          word mode

          contractions are ranked with every other word. punctuation and capitals land on about one word in five each, at random. none of it changes which word is tracked.

          sentence length

          short is 3 to 6 words, medium 5 to 12, long 10 to 30, and any has no limit. a run still ends on a whole sentence.

          vocabulary

          how many rare words a sentence may hold. rare means beyond the 3000 most frequent words. common only allows none, and mostly common allows about one word in seven.

          run length

          sentence runs take whole sentences, so they overshoot rather than cut one short.

          sudden death reps

          clean passes you owe before a missed word lets you go.

          250ms

          how long the keyboard stays locked after a wrong key. the cursor is gone for exactly that long.

          keyboard

          decides which keys count as neighbours when a slip is classified.

          theme
          while typing

          zen hides everything but the words while you type. move the mouse to bring it back.
