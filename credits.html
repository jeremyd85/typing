sentences

Every sentence in sentence mode comes from the Tatoeba Project. Sentences are copyright of their authors and are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 2.0 France (legal code). They are used unmodified. The same corpus supplies the word frequencies that order the word list.

The English export of the Tatoeba Project holds 2,039,120 sentences. 1,937,380 of them were eligible: they have a recorded author, use only letters and basic punctuation, start with a capital, and end in a full stop, exclamation mark or question mark. There is no filter on length or on content. 100,000 were drawn at random for this game. 87,551 were drawn first, to give each of the 5,000 most common words at least 20 sentences and every other word at least 5, or every sentence that exists when a word has fewer. The remaining 12,449 were drawn with no condition at all.

Each sentence in the game's data file records the id of the original and the name of its author, and the results screen after a sentence run credits the sentences you typed, each with a link to its original on Tatoeba. 2,441 authors wrote the 100,000 sentences in use.
